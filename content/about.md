Hello, my name is Samuel, a student studying for a bachelor's degree in engineering, information technology in Finland. This is where I come to write about my hobby projects and other interests in my free time. My main topics are cybersecurity, network engineering, and system administration, but you will find topics related to other stuff as well here in my blog. Generally, anything related to tinkering with technology is deserving of its own blog post or article. I personally find documenting my experiences within tech to be a rewarding experience on top of reinforcing my understanding. 

 I created this blog, because I wanted to document my learning journey by writing quality and in depth content, while at the same time, maybe helping some people on the way. It's purpose is also to show proof of competence in my areas of interest. In my blog posts, I tend to go pretty heavy on the storytelling, but I suppose I'm just trying to soften up what otherwise tend to be very dull technical write-ups. I do occasionally write article style posts as well, which take more emphasis on formal writing and authority, and may thus be a little denser than my usual blog posts.

Despite the mascot I use as my profile picture, I'm shockingly not an Apple Macintosh in real life! I try to keep my public details in this blog to a minimum, but rest assured my name is real and not a pseudonym. Regardless, I always try to remain as transparent as I can in my writing. I seek to minimize ambiguity of interpretation in my blog posts by providing enough detail when necessary, and by citing my sources. If no sources are provided for a claim, then you'll know they are my own opinion or conclusion and should be taken as is. In case you have any questions, suggestions or feedback, please feel free to leave a comment in any of my posts.
