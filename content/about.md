Hello, my name is Samuel, a student studying for a bachelor's degree in engineering, information technology in Finland. This is where I come to write about my hobby projects in my free time. My main interests are cybersecurity, RF engineering and system administration, but you will find topics related to other stuff as well here in my blog. Generally anything related to tinkering with technology is deserving of its own blog. I personally find documenting my experiences within tech to be a rewarding experience on top of reinforcing my understanding of topics. 

In my blogs, I tend to go pretty heavy on the storytelling side of things, but I guess I'm just trying to soften up what otherwise tend to be very dull technical write ups. I suppose it's also a way of honing my English writing skills. I won't go into too much depth about my personal details or my past work experience here, since I tend to be privacy oriented in that way. The main purpose of this blog is to show proof of competence in my areas of interest and to provide high quality and in depth information, which I hope you find useful in some way! If you have any questions, suggestions or feedback, please feel free to leave a comment in any of my posts.
