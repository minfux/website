Hello, my name is Samuel, a student studying for a bachelor's degree in engineering, information technology in Finland. This is where I come to write about my hobby projects in my free time. My main interests are cybersecurity, network engineering and system administration, but you will find topics related to other stuff as well here in my blog. Generally, anything related to tinkering with technology is deserving of its own blog post. I personally find documenting my experiences within tech to be a rewarding experience on top of reinforcing my understanding of topics. 

In my blogs, I tend to go pretty heavy on the storytelling side of things, but I guess I'm just trying to soften up what otherwise tend to be very dull technical write-ups. I suppose it's also a way of honing my English writing skills. The main purpose of this blog is to show proof of competence in my areas of interest and to provide high quality and in depth information, which I hope you find useful in some way! 

Despite the mascot I use as my profile picture, I'm shockingly not an Apple Macintosh in real life! I try to keep my public details in this blog to a minimum, but rest assured my name is real and not a pseudonym. Regardless, I always try to remain as transparent as I can in my writing. I also seek to minimize any ambiguity of interpretation in my blog posts by providing as much detail as necessary. All sources are cited as hyperlinks in my writing. If no sources are provided for a claim, then you'll know they are my own opinion and should be taken as is. In case you have any questions, suggestions or feedback, please feel free to leave a comment in any of my posts.
